
## Workspace Goal

This workspace aims to develop a constructed language with these goals (from highest priority to lowest, in case conflict):

i. Beautiful to hear, suited for music, poetry and novel
ii. The language should be neutral(can be inspired from different language), but must ensure its word and grammar not like other language too much(avoid loan word from other language)
iii. Typable on an English keyboard without custom symbols (or at least can be type by some language keyboard)
iv. Must sound beautiful specifically across: epic/powerful power metal; emotionally delicate post-rock; resonant symphonic vocals; ethereal nostalgic folk.
