# 01 — Phonology (LOCKED)

Status: locked. Numbering (`V*`, `C*`, `P*`, `N1`) matches the original discussion.

## 1. Consonants — 16 phonemes / 19 letters (17 letters in the original design; `/t͡ʃ/` `c` and `/j/` `j` were added later)

| Class | Phonemes | Spelling | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stops | `/b d g/` (+ free `[p t k]`) | `b d g` (hard spelling `p t k`) | **3 phonemes, not 6.** Dictionary (citation) form uses the VOICED member `b d g` (C2). The voiceless `p t k` is the "hard" performance variant (C2b) — a realisational choice, **not** a phoneme. See the phonation note below the table. |
| Nasals | `/m n ŋ/` | `m n ng` | `ng` = /ŋ/ may **never** be word-initial. Because the coda set excludes /ŋ/, `/ŋ/` shows up only as an onset between vowels (`songa` OK, `nga` NO). |
| Tap | `/r/` = [ɾ] | `r` | Single tap. Geminate `rr` is expected to surface as a trill [r] (a free bonus). |
| Lateral | `/l/` | `l` | |
| Fricatives | `/f v s ʃ θ h/` | `f w s sh th h` | `w` = /v/ (German-style). `sh` = /ʃ/, `th` = /θ/ (as in *theta*, voiceless). |
| Affricate | `/t͡ʃ/` | `c` | **Added later.** Onset-only; the voicing knob does NOT apply. |
| Approximant | `/j/` | `j` | **Added later.** A standalone phoneme (not a voicing-pair member). **Onset-only**: `j` must be followed by a V (`ja`) or a C (`jm`, `jn`), so it is never a coda — this prevents any clash with the i-off-glide diphthongs (`tai` = diphthong; `taj` is illegal). |

> **NOTE — stop voicing is PHONATION, not a phoneme contrast** (clarifies C2/C2b; author's ruling).
> The six stop *letters* `p b t d k g` spell **three phonemes** `/b d g/`, each with a free
> voiceless realisation `[p t k]`. Voicing therefore never distinguishes two words, so it is not a
> phoneme feature: it is the **hard <-> soft** setting of `02-notation.md` — a **voice-quality
> (phonation)** choice, the same layer that licenses *whispering* (in a whisper every stop is
> realised voiceless, in every position, and no word is lost). Hence it is a
> **performance/notation-register** device, NOT a phonological process — which is exactly why it
> may be **free and unconditioned**, unlike the *conditioned* voicing alternations of natural
> languages (word-final devoicing, sandhi assimilation, rendaku). ⚠️ **AMENDED by P6 (2026-10-14):
> there is now exactly ONE conditioned environment** — the phoneme `/g/` after a coda `n` is
> obligatorily `[k]`, written `nk` — so the knob is free everywhere *except* there. The author
> deliberately wanted this first taste of a natural-language-style devoicing (清化); whether `/b/`
> and `/d/` after a nasal coda follow it is still open (`03-open-questions.md` §0 item 25).
> Three boundaries apply:
> - **(R-a) Domain = the 3 stops only.** `w` (=/v/) is a **real phoneme** and must **never** be
>   hardened: `f` vs `w` is a true contrast, so devoicing `w` -> `f` would destroy words. `c`
>   (/t͡ʃ/) has no partner; fricatives, nasals and sonorants are untouched.
> - **(R-b) Base forms always use the voiced letter.** No root may be coined with `p t k` in its
>   base form, or its soft form would collide with another root's hard form (a homophone). This
>   also protects the ~16-consonant affix-identity alphabet. ⚠️ **AMENDED by P6:** the fixed sequence
>   **`nk`** (= coda `n` + the `/g/` phoneme) is a **required** hard spelling inside a base form. The
>   collision this rule guards against cannot occur there (`nka` = /n.g…/ vs `nga` = /ŋ…/ are
>   different phoneme sequences).
> - **(R-c) Register boundary.** Plain text and the dictionary are voiced **only**; hard spellings
>   live in notation/performance text and in actual singing. A lookup therefore never misses:
>   `losdo` is the headword, `losto` its hard realisation (P5). ⚠️ **AMENDED by P6:** `nk` is a
>   *required* hard spelling, so the letter `k` may appear in the dictionary form — but only inside
>   that fixed sequence, and it is a **spelling rule**, not a register choice (the performer still
>   chooses freely how to voice it, unless P6's open sub-question is settled the other way).
> **Count consistency:** **16** is the figure already used in `05` (`|C| = 16 distinctive
> consonants`; and `pr br tr dr kr gr` = **3** distinctive clusters, since `p/b`, `t/d`, `k/g` are
> one phoneme each), and `04`'s "14" is the same rule on the older 17-letter inventory. **No
> capacity number changes.**
> **Proper-noun exemption (author's ruling):** personal/proper names keep their historical spelling
> and are **not** bound by C2 — so the name `austareus` (`aus-ta-reus`, with a voiceless `t`) is
> legal as it stands and is **not** respelled. A performer may still harden or soften such a name
> freely, like any other word.

**Deliberately absent:** `/w/`-as-phoneme, `/z/`, `/ð/`, `/ʒ/`.
The letter `v` is **reserved for the vibrato mark** (see `02-notation.md`), which is why
the sound /v/ is written `w`.

> **UPDATE — later additions** (this supersedes the earlier draft, which excluded `/j/`
> and affricates):
> 1. `/j/` (`j`) and `/t͡ʃ/` (`c`, a single letter — `c` was otherwise unused; this
>    replaces an earlier `ch` idea) are now part of the inventory. Both are **onset-only**.
> 2. **Onset clusters — final set** (after revisions): `pr br tr dr kr gr` **plus**
>    `fm fn hm hn wm wn jm jn` (`w` = /v/). Clusters may occupy an onset slot, either
>    **medially** (`CC`); each occupies 2 places. **Word-initial clusters are limited to the
>    stop+`r` set** (`pr br tr dr kr gr`); `fm fn hm hn wm wn jm jn` are
>    **VCV-only** (intervocalic, never word-initial). (`st` used to be on this list; it was
>    removed — see item 4.)
> 3. **Root shape policy** (lexicon level — see `05-root-shape-limit.md`):
>    `VCV(C) / {C}CVCV(C) / VCCV(C) / {C}CVCCV(C) / CVV(C)`; medial `CC` is either
>    (coda `m n l r s` + onset) **or** a permitted cluster; `CVV` uses only the 4 hiatus
>    pairs `iu yu ia ya`; the word-final `(C)` follows a **hard** area rule
>    (pick one of `{f,v}`; pick one of `{h, no-final}`).
>    **Author clarification:** these five shapes enumerate the **2-syllable core only**.
>    **1-syllable roots are frequent** (`roi`) and **3-syllable roots are rare**
>    (`argonna`); `CVV(C)` means any **legal** VV (a diphthong or a hiatus pair). The 1- and
>    3-syllable enumerations are **still to be written**.
> 4. **P5 — `st` is NOT a cluster.** It is the ordinary sequence **coda `s` + onset
>    `t`/`d`** (`losdo` = `los-do`). Consequences: the old "VCV-only / must not touch
>    another consonant" stipulation disappears, because it is now **automatic** (a word
>    cannot begin with a coda, and 3 consonants between vowels are not a legal shape); the
>    voicing knob applies normally (`losdo` ~ `losto`); and **no syllable ever begins with
>    `st`** — every syllable starts with a single consonant or one of the 11 clusters above.
> 5. **P6 — the `sh` / `ng` reading, and the `nk` rule.** Medial `sh` and `ng` are **always one
>    phoneme** (`sh` = /ʃ/, `ng` = /ŋ/). The competing coda+onset readings (`as-ha`, `son-ga`) were
>    also legal but **spelled identically**, so one spelling covered two phoneme sequences — in 9.8%
>    of the coining pool — and a score could not tell the singer which phoneme to sing. **Fix: a
>    wanted coda `n` + onset `/g/` sequence MUST be written `nk`** (the hard letter of /g/), which
>    makes the spelling→phoneme map bijective again. `nk` is **not** a cluster: it is coda `n` +
>    onset `k`, read exactly as P5 reads `st`. `/s/` + `/h/` has no such escape and is therefore
>    **unspellable (illegal)** — no suffix begins with `h`, so morphology can never need it.
>    ⚠️ **R-b / R-c are amended** (see §1): `nk` is a required hard spelling, so it appears in the
>    dictionary too — a *spelling rule*, not a register choice. ⚠️ **DECIDED (author, 2026-10-14):
>    the devoicing is REAL, not spelling-only** — `/g/` after a coda `n` is obligatorily `[k]`, so
>    C2b's "free and unconditioned" knob is **blocked in that one environment** (the language's first
>    genuine phonological process). Full consequences: `00-README.md` **P6**; its scope for `/b/`,
>    `/d/` is open (`03-open-questions.md` §0 item 25).

## 2. Vowels — 7 phonemes

```
            front        central      back
close       i   y                      u
close-mid   e                          o
near-open   ae
open                                   a
```

| Spelling | IPA | Note |
|---|---|---|
| `i` | /i/ | |
| `e` | /e/ | |
| `y` | /y/ | **the lone front-rounded vowel** (no `/ø/`) — the language's signature sound |
| `ae` | /æ/ | a **digraph**; length is written `aee` (repeat last letter) |
| `a` | /ɑ/ | note: a *back* open vowel, deliberately "dark" |
| `o` | /o/ | close-mid, symmetric with `e` |
| `u` | /u/ | |

Design theme: the whole system is a **bright <-> dark palette** —
bright front {`i`, `e`, `y`, `ae`} vs dark back {`u`, `o`, `a`}. This is the main
"texture knob" that serves the four target genres (bright for folk / post-rock,
dark for epic / power metal).

> **This palette is also the carrier of the grammar:** the vowel-colour ("grade") system reads a suffix
> vowel as a **relational contrast** against the root's last vowel (same / the class's central vowel /
> the opposite pole). See `00-README.md`'s terminology warning and the H1–H8 rows. ⚠️ The language has
> **no tone/pitch** — these are colour classes, not musical pitches.

## 3. Diphthongs — 8

Rule: **nucleus = a non-high vowel {a, ae, e, o}; off-glide = {i, u}**.
High vowels {i, u, y} can never be a diphthong nucleus. The off-glides are **not**
separate phonemes.

| `ai` | `au` | `ei` | `eu` | `oi` | `ou` | `aei` | `aeu` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| /ɑi̯/ | /ɑu̯/ | /ei̯/ | /eu̯/ | /oi̯/ | /ou̯/ | /æi̯/ | /æu̯/ |

## 4. Hiatus ("leaps") — 4

Two adjacent vowels in **separate syllables**. Only these four exist, and they form a
clean 2x2 (front-high x extreme-back):

| | `-u` | `-a` |
|---|---|---|
| `i-` | `iu` /i.u/ | `ia` /i.ɑ/ |
| `y-` | `yu` /y.u/ | `ya` /y.ɑ/ |

Musically this is meaningful: a **diphthong = one syllable = one smooth note**, while a
**hiatus = two syllables = a possible leap/interval**. Reference words agree:
`aurorius` = `au-ro-ri-us` (diphthong `au` + hiatus `iu`), `austareus` = `aus-ta-reus`
(diphthongs `au` and `eu`; **no leap**). *(Corrected: an earlier draft read `Au-sta-re-us`,
which assumed an `e|u` hiatus — impossible, since `e`+`u` is one of the legal diphthongs.
Note the examples are lowercase: capitals mean **stress** (V6/N1), so `Au-…` would wrongly
read as stressed.)*

## 5. Phonotactics

- **Syllable skeleton:** `(C)V(C)` — with the whitelisted onset clusters below.
- **Onset:** a single consonant, OR a permitted cluster.
- **Permitted onset clusters (P2), extensible list:**
  - `pr br tr dr kr gr` — may appear in **any** onset position (word-initial OK), but
    must **not touch another consonant** (previous syllable must be open, i.e. `a-tra`
    OK, `an-tra` NO) and may **never be word-final**. Voicing is free (`tr`~`dr`),
    dictionary form uses the voiced form.
  - `fm fn hm hn wm wn jm jn` (`w` = /v/) — **VCV only**: only between two vowels (never
    word-initial). No stop is involved, so the voicing knob never applies to them.
  - `st` — **NOT a cluster** (P5). It is simply coda `s` + onset `t`/`d` (`losdo` =
    `los-do`, `losto` = `los-to`); it never occupies one onset slot, and no syllable can
    begin with it.
  - `sp` and `sk` are **excluded** (author dislikes them).
- **Coda (P3):** only `m n l r s`.
- **Geminates:** `ss nn ll mm rr ww` — **intervocalic only**, never word-final
  (`argonna` OK, `argonn` NO). `argonna` = `ar-gon-na` is valid.
- **Word-final — TWO layers (author's ruling 2026-10-14; A1 and R1 are NOT in conflict):**
  - a **bare root** may end in `∅` (a vowel) **or** `f` / `v` / `h` — this is `R1`'s **area rule**;
    it may **not** end in `m n l r s`. **Mechanism (author's ruling 2026-10-14):** the area rule is
    **two independent binary choices** — one picks a **fricative** from `{f, v}`, the other picks
    **either `h` or nothing** — so every base yields **exactly 2** bare roots: one from the
    `{f,v}` branch and one from the `{h,∅}` branch (base `lafi` → `lafif`/`lafiw`, and `lafih`/`lafi`).
    **Spelling:** the `/v/` member is written `w` — `v` is the *vibrato* mark and not a letter, so a
    /v/-final root is `…w` (e.g. `lafiw`), never `…v` (see `02-notation.md`).
    The raw *form* space is therefore 4 per base, while the **coined** space is **2 per base**.
    The two siblings differ in one segment only, so a pair is **exempt** from the
    distance-`>= 2` guideline (R2: the area rule is *hard*, the consonant-distance guideline *soft*).
    **The branch choice is NOT free texture (author's ruling, 2026-10-14 = `R3`):** the branch
    carries **grammatical meaning** — the root's final consonant marks a **class** whose content is
    still to be defined (`00-README.md` R3; `03-open-questions.md` §0 item 14). The only *free* choice
    left is within a branch (`f` vs `w`; `h` vs no final). Note the two siblings **share the same H4
    trigger** (a coda never changes the last-syllable nucleus), so a pair is grammatically identical
    except for the class;
  - **any word** (i.e. including every suffixed / derived form) may end in `∅`, `f`, `v`, `h`
    **or** `m n l r s` (the P3 coda set);
  - voiced/voiceless stops never end a word (`p t k` are performance spellings of `b d g` anyway — C2);
  - consequence: `los` is **not** a legal bare root while `laf` is, and `-ner` / `-ser` are legal
    because they are *word*-final, not *bare-root*-final.
- **No adjacent vowels** beyond sections 3 and 4: if two vowels would meet illegally,
  a consonant must separate them. (This is a hard constraint that morphology must
  satisfy — see the morphology open questions.)

## 6. Stress (P4, P4c, P4d, N1)

- Stress is **not lexical**: the dictionary form carries no stress mark. It is a
  **performance choice** (the composer may rewrite it).
- **Default:** the **2nd syllable counted from the root**; if the root has only one
  syllable, stress falls on it.
- **Affixes never move the stress** (root-based counting).
- **Compounds** keep only the **last root's** stress; earlier roots are de-stressed
  (implies a head-final tendency). **⚠️ PROVISIONAL** — the author has flagged this as a temporary
  working assumption to be re-tested once more grammar exists (it was also the (circular) justification
  once offered for choosing suffixes). See `00-README.md` P4d.
- In notation the stress is written as a CAPITAL on the **first letter of the stressed
  syllable** (`losdo` -> `losDo`; `argonna` -> `arGonna`, hardened `arKonna`).
